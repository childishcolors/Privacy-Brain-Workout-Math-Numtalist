## 1. Information Collection and Use

For a better experience, while using our App, we may require you to provide us with certain personally identifiable information or allow device-level tracking.

### A. Third-Party Services (AdMob & Personalized Ads)
Our App integrates Google AdMob to display banner and interstitial advertisements. To serve relevant and personalized advertisements, Google AdMob collects and processes unique identifiers from your device, including:
* **IP Address**
* **Mobile Advertising Identifiers** (e.g., Apple IDFA - Identifier for Advertisers)
* **App usage and interaction data** (such as ad impressions and clicks)
* **Device Characteristics** (e.g., model, OS version, language settings)

For more information, please read the Google AdMob privacy policies:
* [Google Privacy & Terms](https://policies.google.com/privacy)
* [How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites)

## 2. App Tracking Transparency (ATT) (iOS Users)

In compliance with Apple's iOS policies, if you use an iOS device, you will be prompted to grant permission for App Tracking Transparency (ATT). 
* **If you consent (Allow):** Google AdMob will use your device's IDFA to serve personalized, high-quality, and highly relevant advertisements.
* **If you decline (Ask App Not to Track):** You will still see advertisements, but they will not be personalized or tailored to your interests and will be served as contextual ads.

You can modify your tracking preferences at any time in your iOS device settings by going to:
`Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking`

---

## 3. Data Stored On-Device (Local Data)

Numtalist is designed with user privacy in mind. Most of your core application data is stored locally on your physical device and never uploaded to any remote server:
* **Streak & Level Progress**: Saved directly in your device's secure Local Storage.
* **XP & Quiz Scores**: Managed fully on-device.
* **Alarm / Reminder Settings**: Set locally via Capacitor native local notifications.

You can completely wipe all of this data from your device at any time by navigating to `Settings` inside the App and selecting **"Reset all progress"**.
